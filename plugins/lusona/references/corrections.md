# Correction limits

Read before correcting or probing an observed failed check or review finding. Expected red in a test-first step is not a failure and does not start this procedure.

## Correction rounds

- Outside a batch, a correction round is one bounded correction or diagnostic probe for an observed failure, followed by its agreed verification.
- The first attempt is not a correction round.
- For each failure, keep two counts: consecutive rounds without material progress, and total rounds.
- Keep the counts across leaves, roles and handoffs.
- Record both counts and the evidence in the owning record. Put the counts in its current handoff.
- Material progress resolves part of the failure, or establishes a new fact that changes the next action.
- A rerun, changed wording or a different leaf is not material progress.
- Only demonstrated material progress resets the consecutive count. Nothing resets the total.
- Inside a batch, apply the batch's recorded repair budget instead of these counts.

## Limits

1. After 2 consecutive rounds without material progress, stop correcting that failure. Report the evidence, the ruled-out causes, and the missing input or approach.
2. Continue authorized work that the failure does not affect.
3. While the total is below 4, you may start a materially different bounded approach within existing authority. It requires new evidence or a changed prerequisite.
4. Before that approach, state the change, its hypothesis, its stopping condition and the expected evidence.
5. A new label on unchanged inputs and evidence is not a different approach.
6. After one more round without material progress, stop the affected work again.
7. After 4 total rounds, stop the affected work. Ask the user before any further round or approach on that failure.
8. Ask the user for a missing decision or authority; as a leaf, report it to the coordinator. Inside a batch, defer the item under the batch rules. Do not invent a reset.
