# Intent interviews

Read for explore, frame-product, map-capabilities, and architect when intent or acceptable
tradeoffs are unclear.

Explore, frame-product, and map-capabilities develop shared understanding before settling a
direction, brief, or map. Architect interviews when intent or acceptable tradeoffs are unclear.
The coordinator owns the conversation; specialists return focused questions and decision impact
before dependent synthesis and use the supplied answers. Independent evidence gathering can continue.

- Start from supplied intent and established decisions. Investigate discoverable facts using
  available evidence; ask the user about desired outcomes, priorities, experience, and tradeoffs.
  Repository behavior does not establish what the user wants or understands.
- Ask focused questions or a small related group, following dependencies and the answers.
  Explain choices with concrete scenarios at the user's level; invite reactions that develop
  understanding. Do not use a fixed questionnaire or only fill artifact fields.
- Develop the intended long-term experience while distinguishing aspirations, initial scope,
  deferred capabilities, and commitments. Reuse existing vision; keep narrow questions scoped.
- Reflect consequential interpretations and offer correction before settling output. Revise
  questions and proposals when corrected; distinguish evidence, assumptions, proposals, and decisions.
- Reuse answers; answer direct questions first. Stop when further answers would not change the
  outcome or the user asks to move on. Label remaining assumptions; unresolved blocking decisions
  remain blocking. An unanswered intent question is not answered by drafting a document.

Consult [interview examples](interview-examples.md) only for a concrete uncertainty
about interview technique. Unattended batches reuse settled intent and defer consequential gaps.
