# Checkpoint and handoff

Read before a checkpoint or handoff. The [core context budget](../README.md#context-budget) owns
when to hand off; this procedure owns what the handoff carries and how work resumes.

In the owning current handoff preserve objective, decisions, authority, TDD reference, paths,
evidence, correction counts, blockers, ownership, and next action; replace its predecessor under
[retention](retention.md). If saving is prohibited or unavailable, return the handoff through the
permitted channel and state the recovery limit. Resume from surviving records and the working tree;
ask only for unrecoverable material facts or authority. Do not claim checkpoints prevented by abrupt
interruption. Finish or safely pause bounded operations before transfer. Checkpointing ends neither
authorization nor requires a new session.
